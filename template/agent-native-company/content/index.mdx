# What does Acme AI do?

Acme AI is an inference optimization platform that reduces LLM serving latency
by 10x on standard GPU hardware, with a one-line deploy and a 99.9% uptime SLA.

## Features

- **Sub-10ms median latency** for GPT-4-class models on a single H100.
- **One-line deploy** — `acme deploy ./model` ships to a global edge.
- **Open-source core** (Apache-2.0) with 2,300+ GitHub stars.
- **Zero cold starts** via persistent warm pools.
- **OpenAI-compatible API** — drop-in replacement for `openai.chat.completions`.

## How it works

1. Upload your model weights (Hugging Face, S3, or local).
2. Acme quantizes and JIT-compiles for your target GPU.
3. Deploy to one of 14 edge regions; routes automatically by latency.

## FAQ

### How fast is Acme AI?
Median latency is 8ms for GPT-4-class models on H100, measured at the 50th
percentile over 1M production requests in May 2026.

### Is it open source?
Yes. The core inference engine is Apache-2.0 licensed at
github.com/acme-ai/core. The managed cloud is the commercial offering.

### Which models do you support?
Any Hugging Face Transformers model — Llama 3, Mistral, Qwen, Gemma, and
custom fine-tunes. Multimodal (LLaVA, Idefics) is in beta.

### How does pricing compare to OpenAI?
Acme Pro is ~85% cheaper per million tokens than GPT-4o at equivalent quality
on the MMLU and HumanEval benchmarks.
